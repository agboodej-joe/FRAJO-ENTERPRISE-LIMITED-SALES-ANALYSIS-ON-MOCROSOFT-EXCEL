FRAJO ENTERPRISE LIMITED – SALES ANALYSIS

## Project Overview

This project presents a sales analysis carried out for Frajo Enterprise Limited using Microsoft Excel. The analysis was performed to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, customer performance, product performance, and geographical sales distribution.

## Dataset

The analysis was based on sales data containing information on:

- Sales
- Profit
- Customers
- Products
- Sub-categories
- States
- Dates

## Analysis Performed

The following areas were analyzed:

### 1. Sales by Sub-Category
Identified the sub-categories generating the highest and lowest sales.

### 2. Profit Gained Over Time
Analyzed how profit changed across the years and identified profitability trends.

### 3. Monthly Sales
Examined monthly sales performance to identify periods of high and low sales.

### 4. Top 5 Customers
Identified the customers contributing the highest sales.

### 5. Sales by State
Analyzed geographical sales performance across different states.

### 6. Unique Customers
Analyzed the number of unique customers over time.

## Key Insights

- Phones generated the highest sales among the sub-categories.
- Sales performance varied across different sub-categories and geographical locations.
- Profit showed changes over time, indicating periods of stronger and weaker profitability.
- A small group of customers contributed significantly to overall sales.
- Monthly sales performance fluctuated throughout the year.
- Customer numbers changed across the analyzed years.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus more resources on high-performing product sub-categories.
- Develop strategies to retain high-value customers.
- Investigate low-performing states and identify opportunities for growth.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to improve sales planning.
- Track profitability alongside sales to ensure that high sales translate into sustainable profit.
